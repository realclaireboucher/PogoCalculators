Strategy Explanation

This calculator uses the "Walk and Level Up" strategy, where you walk your buddy Pokémon and power it up whenever you have enough candy to reach the next level. This approach is efficient because:

  • As your buddy levels up, the XL Candy drop rate increases
  • Higher level buddies (especially 31+) have significantly better XL Candy rates (~70-80%)
  • You maximize XL Candy gains by keeping your buddy at the highest possible level during the walk

XL Candy Probability from Buddy Walking

Research from The Silph Road determined the XL Candy drop rates from transferring Pokémon based on their level. A follow-up study found that buddy walking appears to use the same rate formula. The probability increases with Pokémon level as shown below:

Pokémon Level Sample Size Observed Rate Probability
Levels 1-14 8,363 0.027 ± 0.003 1/40
Levels 15-19 3,552 0.13 ± 0.01 1/8
Levels 20-22 2,512 0.25 ± 0.02 2/8
Levels 23-25 1,994 0.39 ± 0.02 3/8
Levels 26-30 3,425 0.48 ± 0.02 4/8

This calculator uses these empirically-determined probabilities to estimate XL Candy gains from buddy walking.
